Modifications by Noxari: the 85 original grayscale PNG illustrations were
resized to 384 px, recolored to a single light-blue tint with transparency
derived from the original brightness, and saved as WebP
(public/constellation-art/*.webp). Placement in the sky uses the three anchor
stars published with each illustration. The modified images are distributed
under the same Free Art License 1.3. Original file hashes are listed in
data/stellarium-art/manifest.json; regenerate with
scripts/prepare_constellation_art.py.

Anchor star coordinates: HYG Database v4.1 (CC BY-SA 4.0),
https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database
