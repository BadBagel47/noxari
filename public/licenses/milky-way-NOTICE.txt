Modifications by Noxari: converted to luminance, point stars removed with a
median filter, reprojected from galactic to J2000 equatorial coordinates
(1440 × 720 equirectangular grid), sky floor subtracted, normalised and
gamma-adjusted (src/generated/milkyWay.png). Generator:
scripts/generate_milky_way_eso.py; source hash in data/milky-way-eso/manifest.json.
