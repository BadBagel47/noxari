Noxari — deep-zoom sky images (F-062)

Survey: Digitized Sky Survey (DSS2), colour composite "DSS colored" (HiPS ivo://CDS/P/DSS2/color),
produced at the Space Telescope Science Institute (STScI) under U.S. Government grant NAG W-2166,
coloured and HEALPix-tiled by CDS (Strasbourg; HiPS © CNRS/Unistra), fetched from the ESA ESASky
mirror https://skies.esac.esa.int/DSSColor only when the eye-level view is zoomed in past 3×.
