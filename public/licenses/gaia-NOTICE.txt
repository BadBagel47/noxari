Changes made for Noxari: stars with G <= 11 (public/stars/gaia-v1/) and 11 < G <= 12 in finer
tiles (public/stars/gaia12-v1/, F-065) selected; positions moved from epoch 2016.0 to
J2000 with Gaia proper motions; stars already shipped from HYG removed; positions,
magnitude and BP-RP colour quantised and packed into sky tiles (public/stars/gaia-v1/), with
source_id and parallax in separate detail files. Reproducible with
scripts/generate_gaia_stars.py; provenance in data/gaia-stars/manifest.json.
